Issue #47: Weekend Grace Period Evidence

This page demonstrates the weekend-aware staleness logic for repo commit checks.

Default Threshold Repos (Business Days)

Repos without explicit thresholds use business days (weekdays only). Default: 1 business day warning, 2 business days error.
